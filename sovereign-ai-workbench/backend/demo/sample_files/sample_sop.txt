STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: #SOP-2025-07
TITLE: REFINERY PIPELINE INSPECTION & OPERATIONAL CLEARANCE POLICY
ORGANIZATION: NOVA INDUSTRIAL SYSTEMS

SECTION 4.2: WALL THINNING & PRESSURE BOUND TOLERANCES
1. Wall thinning <= 10.0%: Unconditional operational clearance.
2. Wall thinning > 10.0% and <= 15.0%: Conditional operational clearance permitted subject to scheduled seal replacement within 30 days and monthly ultrasonic inspection logs.
3. Wall thinning > 15.0%: Immediate shutdown required for emergency line replacement.

SECTION 5.1: APPROVAL REQUIREMENTS
All conditional clearance notes must be verified against ultrasonic logs and signed by the Chief Mechanical Engineer.
